# Voice of Customer (čeština): how Czech SME owners and managers talk about manual work, overload, digitalisation and AI

Scope note for the report writer. The richest primary source by far is the AMSP ČR "Rok AI 2026" qualitative study (Tempo Ventures, online focus group of 20 owners, managing directors and senior managers of firms with 2–249 staff, run 11–18 March 2026, report April 2026). The report says its quotes are verbatim, exactly as respondents typed them ("Citace jsou doslovné, jak je respondenti napsali"). Respondent tags are the report's nicknames plus its descriptors: firm size / AI adoption level / sector. Quotes are reproduced with the respondents' original spelling and typos. [Tempo/AMSP qual report](https://www.amsp.cz/media/amsp/16307/vysledky-tempo-report-ke-kvalitativnimu-vyzkumu-pro-rok-ai-2026.pdf)

Caveats on that source:
- The report says the panel was "aktivnější a angažovanější než průměrný podnikatel". Each respondent was paid 700 Kč (1,000 Kč for the five most active), or could take a consultation worth 2,500 Kč. N=20, so it is not representative. [Tempo/AMSP qual report](https://www.amsp.cz/media/amsp/16307/vysledky-tempo-report-ke-kvalitativnimu-vyzkumu-pro-rok-ai-2026.pdf)
- Tempo Ventures is itself an AI implementation studio ("zavádíme AI do firemních procesů"), so headline framing such as "Nechci další kurz, chci aby někdo přišel..." may favour a consulting answer. The verbatims are usable; treat the headline framing with caution. [Tempo/AMSP qual report](https://www.amsp.cz/media/amsp/16307/vysledky-tempo-report-ke-kvalitativnimu-vyzkumu-pro-rok-ai-2026.pdf)

Access limits during this research:
- reddit.com and idnes.cz are blocked for the research tool, so r/czech, r/podnikani and iDNES/Ekonom could not be searched.
- podnikatel.cz discussion threads, Facebook groups and podcast transcripts did not surface in search.
- Forum-style VoC is therefore thin. The bank leans on the AMSP study plus a few media interviews.

## 1. How Czech owners describe their pain in their own words (manual work, administration, overload, people, e-mail, Excel)

### Takeaway
The owners' own word for the pain is **"operativa"**: e-mails, orders, staff questions and paperwork for the accountant. They describe it as eating 80 % of their time and pushing strategy into weekends. People shortage is framed as a lack of *good* people, not of hands to replace. "Nestíhám" and "žrout času" appear verbatim. Several of the phrases in the brief ("papírování", "přepisování faktur", "všechno jde přese mě", "zákazník čeká") were **not** found verbatim from owners in 2023–2026 sources. They appear only in association, media or vendor voice (see Gaps).

### Cited Findings

Takže na strategické posouvání firmy zbývá času málo nebo žádný." ### Inferences
- The owners' own vocabulary for the pain is: **operativa, rutina / rutinní činnosti, žrout času, e-maily (kterých chodí nadmíra), dotazy (zákazníků i pracovníků), objednávky, podklady pro účetní, nestíhám, nemám čas, být v běhu, posouvání po drobných krůčcích, firma stagnuje.** These are stronger copy anchors than "administrativní zátěž" or "procesy".
- "Opakující se dotazy" and "nemusí se psát vždy znova" map directly onto FainStudio's inbound-inquiry automation. "Kolegyně to má pod palcem" is the reassurance owners themselves use: a person stays in control.
- The people pain is about a shortage of *good* people and wanting to handle more with the same headcount. Promise "zvládnete víc se stejnými lidmi", not "nahradíte lidi".
- "Lidé = zdroje = finanční prostředky" suggests owners translate time savings into money on their own. Copy can do that arithmetic for them (hours per month times wage).
- Tool fatigue ("nástroj bez procesu je k ničemu", cancelled licences) is a likely objection to any new tool. Positioning a one-off build *inside the existing process* (their e-mail, their Excel, their accounting software) likely answers it better than another app.

## 2. How they talk about AI and automation: enthusiasm vs scepticism, staff reactions, "nevím, kde začít"

### Takeaway
Owners rarely say "hype" or "bublina". Their sceptic vocabulary is **"trend", "módní výstřelek", "velký respekt", "nedůvěra", "spolehnout se nedovolím", "halucinace / nesmysly", "je potřeba korekce", "citlivá data do AI nedáváme"**. Their enthusiasm vocabulary is **"pomocník", "parťák", "zbavuje opičí práce", "ušetří čas", "za 2 hoďky"**. The dominant barrier, in their words, is **"nedostatek fantazie", "nevím, k čemu AI využít", "nevím, jak to udělat"**: not fear of AI itself. Several owners have been burned by over-sold AI ("nahradí živé lidi", "stojí strašně moc peněz a nic nám to nepřináší").

- „Pro mě osobně je AI v současnosti trend. Vím, že mě nemine, že mi může pomoc, ale mám z něho velký respekt." (libuse, mikro, opatrný, energetika) [Tempo/AMSP](https://www.amsp.cz/media/amsp/16307/vysledky-tempo-report-ke-kvalitativnimu-vyzkumu-pro-rok-ai-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Owners already use "halucinace", "kontrola" and "korekce" themselves. Copy can name the risk plainly and answer it ("každý výstup zkontroluje člověk / kolegyně to má pod palcem") rather than hide it. Numbers-heavy automations (invoices, orders, prices) need the strongest control message, given liborf's "u čísel moje nedůvěra je stále velmi vysoká".
- The "nevím, kde začít" barrier is best answered with **concrete examples from their own process**, not education. FainStudio's productised list (poptávky, faktury, objednávky, smlouvy, ceny konkurence) works as a "fantazie" prosthetic: a menu of use cases.
- "Pomocník" and "parťák" are owner-native positive framings. "Zbavuje opičí práce" is a vivid owner phrase worth testing in a headline.
- The over-sold-vendor scars ("nahradit živé lidi", "stojí strašně moc a nic nám to nepřináší", "mysleli jsme, že to bude koukat, jak pracujeme") suggest FainStudio should explicitly *under*-promise: one specific task, a measurable before and after, and a human in the loop.
- For any customer-facing bot (inquiry handling, document chat), the handover to a human is the known pain point. Copy should promise "když si neví rady, předá to člověku".

### Gaps
- **"bublina"** and **"hype"**: not found as owner verbatims. "Módní bublina" appears only in Ipsos's analytical summary, and "AI je přeceňovaný a jenom trend" is a survey answer option. The owner words are "trend" and "módní výstřelek". Hypothesis to verify.
- **"ChatGPT nám psal nesmysly"**: not found verbatim. Nearest is skippy's "až do úplných nesmyslů typu halucinací". Hypothesis to verify.
- **"nechci, aby za nás mluvil robot"**: owner-side verbatim not found. Only customer-side survey data (Mplus) and the AMSP "robot" commentary. Hypothesis to verify, especially for e-shops and services worried about brand voice.
- Explicit owner fear of *staff* backlash ("lidi se budou bát o místo") was not found verbatim in SME sources. The survey says resistance is low. Hypothesis to verify.

## 3. Words that build trust in a vendor vs buzzwords and Anglicisms that put owners off

### Takeaway
The verbatim evidence points to trust being earned by **specificity, a visible price, measurable payback and someone who comes and looks at *my* process**. The strongest distrust trigger found is an **opaque, inflated quote after long "analysis"**: one owner suspects vendors "vydělávají na neznalosti nás zákazníků". The specific trust words in the brief ("férově", "bez keců", "na klíč", "cena předem", "garance") and the buzzwords ("AI-powered", "transformace", "synergie", "řešení na míru") were **not** found in owner verbatims. They stay hypotheses. Anglicisms are used freely by "průkopník" owners and not by cautious ones, so the effect depends on the segment.

## 4. Who in the firm pushes for change, and how they describe convincing the owner

### Takeaway
In Czech SMEs the **owner is both the engine and the brake**: "Majitel může být největší tahoun i největší brzda." Where the owner engages personally, things move. Elsewhere change comes from a self-appointed **"AI ambasador"** or an enthusiastic employee who "infects" colleagues, or from an **external person who shows a concrete example**. Champions argue with simple **payback maths** (licence cost vs hours saved). Their frustration is that leadership "prostě zatím nechtějí ať je matematika jakkoliv jasná".

### Inferences
- For micro and small firms the buyer is effectively the owner. The page must convince the owner directly, in owner language (time, money, people, control).
- In 50+ staff firms the likely champion is a manager or "AI ambasador" (výroba, energetika) who needs an **argument kit for the owner or board**: cost vs hours saved, a fixed price, and a small first project. FainStudio could publish this as a "spočítejte si to" block or a one-page summary the champion can forward.
- "Show, don't tell" is the conversion mechanism in owners' own stories ("když mi to někdo ukázal", "první úspěchy"). Short demo videos of a real inquiry or invoice being processed likely beat claims. Inference; verify.

### Gaps
- No verbatim evidence on **office managers, operations managers or sales heads** specifically as change champions in Czech SMEs. Only "manažer nebo ředitel" (majkl), a self-appointed "AI ambasador" (roland) and enthusiastic colleagues (libuse). Hypothesis to verify: who books the first call (owner vs office manager vs sales head).
- No verbatim found of a champion describing *how they pitched* the owner, beyond the payback maths and the "matematika jakkoliv jasná" frustration.
